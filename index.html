How to Train

  • 3 sets of 8–12 reps on every machine, with 60–90 seconds rest between sets.
  • Go up in weight when you hit 12 reps on all 3 sets. Write the weight down so you can beat it next week.
  • Training early? A banana before is enough fuel. Eat your porridge as soon as you're home, because that's your main recovery meal.
  • Warm up with 5 minutes on the bike or treadmill, then one light set on the first machine.
  • Finish with 15 minutes of incline walking on the treadmill. This helps burn fat without hurting recovery.
  • Leave 1–2 reps in the tank on most sets. Stop if anything feels sharp or painful.

Food Rules

  • Hit about 140g protein a day (about 1.6g per kg). Every day in this plan lands between 115g and 140g.
  • Lunch is a café sandwich. Chicken, egg or ham fillings give the most protein. Avoid cheese-and-mayo-heavy ones.
  • Curry pots 3 nights a week. They cost the most per meal, so beans, pasta and eggs fill the other nights.
  • Pot noodle once a week. It's the lowest-protein dinner, so don't skip the 2 eggs.
  • Stir in frozen veg to the pasta and curry. It's cheap, needs no chopping, and adds fibre.
  • Drink 2.5–3 litres of water a day, plus extra on training days.
  • Weigh in every 2 weeks at the same time of day. Aim to lose 0.3–0.5 kg a week. If you're losing faster, add a slice of toast with peanut butter.
